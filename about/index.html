About Medico Overseas

Built by counsellors who answer the phone

Every year, thousands of NEET-qualified students look beyond India for an affordable path to a medical degree. The difficult part is not finding options — it is knowing which options are recognised, realistic and right for the student.

Medico Overseas is structured to guide students and parents through the full journey: country and university comparison, documentation, admission, visa support and planning for licensing exams after graduation.

Our approach is simple: explain the trade-offs clearly, verify what can be verified, and never push a destination just because it is available.

Why families choose us

What our process is designed to protect

The goal is not the fastest admission. It is a decision that still makes sense after the excitement of admission is over.

No donation-led counselling

Options are compared on student fit, eligibility, recognition and total cost — not pressure tactics.

Recognition discussed upfront

Students are encouraged to verify university and regulatory requirements before committing.

Admission only with eligibility

Academic profile, NEET status and destination-specific rules are considered before application.

English-medium clarity

Medium of instruction and clinical communication realities should be understood before choosing a university.

Total-cost planning

Families should compare tuition together with hostel, living, travel, insurance and other required costs.

Licensing support at the end

Studying abroad is one part of the journey; licensing in India must be planned too.

Important: University recognition, fees, eligibility and regulatory rules can change. Verify current official requirements before admission or payment.
