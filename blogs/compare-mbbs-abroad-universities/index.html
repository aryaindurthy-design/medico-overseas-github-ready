How to compare MBBS abroad universities safely

A practical checklist for recognition, curriculum, clinical exposure, total cost and documents before payment.

What to check

  • Recognition and programme eligibility
  • Course duration and internship structure
  • Teaching language and clinical language
  • Affiliated hospitals and patient exposure
  • Complete annual and one-time costs
  • Hostel, food and student-support arrangements
  • Official documents before any payment
Important: University, regulatory, fee and visa information can change. Verify the exact university, programme and current official requirements before admission or payment.
